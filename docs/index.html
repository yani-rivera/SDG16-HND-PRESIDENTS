Sources and notes

Terms: English and Spanish Wikipedia, “List of presidents of Honduras”. Birthplaces researched and validated across several sources by Yanira A. Rivera. Departments: geoBoundaries ADM1 (CC BY 4.0). Neighboring countries: Natural Earth.

Births in Honduras are placed at their department, because many towns are unknown; the card still names the town where it is known. Births abroad sit in their country. Where the department is unknown, the point sits at the centre of Honduras with a dashed ring. Collective governments (councils of ministers, provisional governments, juntas) have no single birthplace and sit on their own node off the north coast. On the timeline, a dashed outline marks a de facto term. The star marks the capital when each term began: Comayagua until 30 October 1880, when Marco Aurelio Soto moved it to Tegucigalpa (before that, the seat of government at times alternated between the two cities). Party labels are as recorded in the source list.
